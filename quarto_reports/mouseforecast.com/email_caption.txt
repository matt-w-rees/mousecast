Estimated relative mouse activity in Winter 2026 (June - August) across each GRDC agro-ecological zone (broad regions defined by similar climate, soils, and farming systems). Estimates consider live-trapping rates, active burrow counts, chew card assessments, the proportion of surveyed paddocks which detected mice and farmer observations (via MouseAlert). Mouse activity cannot be estimated in zones shaded grey as they have not been surveyed since April 2026.
