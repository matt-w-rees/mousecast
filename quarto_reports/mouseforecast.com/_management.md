+ Mouse activity should be monitored through winter in zones with moderate-high activity: (list zones), and prepare to take action. 

+ Monitor mice activity in crops using [chew cards or a walk-through, searching for active mouse burrows and crop damage](https://grdc.com.au/resources-and-publications/resources/mouse-management/monitor-manage/know-your-mouse-numbers).

+ If you detect mice, consider [applying zinc phosphide bait](https://grdc.com.au/resources-and-publications/resources/mouse-management/monitor-manage/mouse-baiting) before crops mature, as bait will become less attractive to mice once grain develops in the head.

+ ZnP25 is the only currently registered bait available everywhere; use in accordance with the label instructions and report any adverse effects via the [APVMA website](https://www.apvma.gov.au/regulation/adverse-experience-reporting-program). 
An emergency permit is currently available for the higher dose ZnP50 bait, check access and requirements on the [GRDC website](https://grdc.com.au/resources-and-publications/resources/mouse-management). 

+ Plan for a clean harvest (minimal grain loss) to reduce mouse food through the stubble.
