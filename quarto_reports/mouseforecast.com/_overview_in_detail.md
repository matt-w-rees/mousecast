+ [Western Australia]{.underline}: High mouse activity has been recorded in WA Northern and WA Sandplain; with crops maturing, consider baiting before grain fill, as zinc phosphide becomes less effective once grain develops on the head.

+ [Adelaide Plains, Yorke & Eyre Peninsulas (SA)]{.underline}: Moderate mouse activity has been recorded in SA Midnorth-Lower Yorke Eyre; continue monitoring as crops mature, and be ready to act before grain fill if activity increases.

+ Elsewhere (Mallee & Wimmera (Vic/SA), Victoria & southern/central NSW and Northern NSW & southern Queensland), mouse numbers likely remain low.
