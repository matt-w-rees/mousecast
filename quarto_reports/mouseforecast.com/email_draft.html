Mouse Forecast

Winter 2026 update

Hi all,

A new Mouse Forecast is available, along with some changes to the way we communicate these updates.

We have launched a new website - www.mouseforecast.com - where you can see the full report with the most up-to-date information.

We will regularly refresh the website as new data comes in (and continue to send these emails at key times of the growing year, the next due in Spring).

A pdf version of the forecast is also attached to this email, please forward on to your relevant network.

Mouse activity estimates are now completely based on quantitative data. Live-trapping, active burrow, and chew card assessments are integrated with farmer observations via MouseAlert. We have kept the same “traffic light” colour coding but shifted to a continuous scale to show detail. We are actively refining statistical methods to improve these forecasts and their presentation. Please let us know if you have any feedback.

Cheers, Matt.

Overview

  • Western Australia: A mouse plague is under way in the Northern (Geraldton) zone. Mouse activity east from Fitzgerald in the Sandplain (Esperance) zone is also at concerning levels. Eastern (Kwinana East) and Central (Kwinana West) zones have overall low mouse activity, but growers here should remain vigilant as there are some patches with higher activity.

  • South Australia: Mouse activity is moderate across Adelaide Plains and Yorke Peninsula. We have observed a decrease in activity from last season, likely due to lower food availability and proactive management during sowing. However, mouse activity could increase as more food is now available.

  • Elsewhere: mouse numbers likely remain low.

  • An emergency permit has been issued by the APVMA for the higher dose ZnP50 bait. Locations with permit access change on a fortnightly basis, based on APVMA-defined mouse activity categories (which differ from our mouse forecasts due to different data types, metrics and time periods). Check the GRDC website for emergency permit information and current access.
