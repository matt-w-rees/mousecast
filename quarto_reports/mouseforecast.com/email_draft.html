Mouse Forecast

Winter 2026 update

Hi all,

A new Mouse Forecast is now available, along with some changes to the way we communicate these updates.

We have launched a new website - mouseforecast.com - where you can see the full report with the most up-to-date information.

Mouse activity estimates are now completely based on quantitative data. Live-trapping, active burrow, and chew card assessments are integrated with farmer observations via MouseAlert. We have kept the same “traffic light” colour coding but shifted to a continuous scale to show detail. We are actively refining statistical methods to improve estimates and their presentation.

We will regularly refresh the website as new data comes in (and continue to send these emails at key times of the growing year). A pdf copy is also attached to this email, feel free to forward on to your relevant network along with the website URL.

Lastly, our thoughts are with those in WA affected by the current mouse plague.

Cheers, Matt.

Dr Matthew Rees

Overview

  • Western Australia: A mouse plague is under way across multiple regions. The worst outbreak is occurring in the Northern (Geraldton) zone, followed by another east from Fitzgerald in the Sandplain (Esperance) zone. Mouse abundance in Eastern (Kwinana East) and Central (Kwinana West) zones appears to be low, but growers here should remain vigilant.

  • South Australia: Mouse activity is moderate across Adelaide Plains and Yorke Peninsula. We have observed a decrease in activity from last season, likely due to lower food availability and proactive management during sowing. However, mouse activity could increase as more food is now available.

  • Elsewhere: mouse numbers likely remain low.

  • An emergency permit has been issued by the APVMA for the higher dose ZnP50 bait. Locations with permit access change on a fortnightly basis, based on APVMA-defined current mouse activity categories. This differs from our mouse forecasts, which are based on different data types, metrics and time periods. Check the GRDC website for emergency permit information and current access.
