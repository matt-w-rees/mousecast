+ [Western Australia]{.underline}: [**A mouse plague is under way across multiple regions**.]{style="color: red;"}
The worst outbreak is occurring in the Northern (Geraldton) zone, followed by another east from Fitzgerald in the Sandplain (Esperance) zone. 
Mouse abundance in Eastern (Kwinana East) / and Central (Kwinana West) zones appears to be low, but growers here should remain vigilant.

+ [South Australia]{.underline}: Mouse activity is moderate across Adelaide Plains and Yorke Peninsula. 
We have observed a decrease in activity from last season, likely due to lower food availability and proactive management during sowing. 
However, mouse activity could increase as more food is now available. 

+ [Elsewhere]{.underline}: mouse numbers likely remain low.

+ [An emergency permit has been issued by the APVMA for the higher dose ZnP50 bait]{.underline}.
Locations with permit access change on a fortnightly basis, based on APVMA-defined current mouse activity categories (differing from our mouse forecasts, which are based on different data types, metrics and time periods).
Check the [GRDC website](https://grdc.com.au/resources-and-publications/resources/mouse-management) for emergency permit information and [current access](https://grdc.com.au/resources-and-publications/resources/mouse-management/aez-lookup/aez-lookup-table-and-map).
