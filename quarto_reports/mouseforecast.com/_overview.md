+ [Western Australia]{.underline}:
  - Northern (Geraldton) region: Mouse numbers have reduced from previous seasons, but recent surveys confirm numbers remain relatively high.
  - Sandplain (Esperance) region: Mouse numbers are expected to be moderate across more than half of the region, likely due to exceptional winter crop growth.
  - Central and Eastern regions: Mouse numbers mostly low.

+ [South Australia]{.underline}:
  - Surveys have found mostly low numbers except on southern Yorke Peninsula, which detected moderate mouse activity.
  - Despite overall low survey results, above-average winter crop growth and a recent swing from long-term dry to wet conditions (which often comes before outbreaks) point to a risk of higher numbers in coming seasons.
  - With no recent surveys in the Far West, Upper Eyre Peninsula or around Jamestown, the model forecasts high numbers there from conditions alone, but with considerable uncertainty (shown with pale red in @fig-phase) - this estimate should be taken with a large grain of salt. 

+ [Victoria]{.underline}:
  - Mallee: Mouse numbers are potentially moderate in parts of the Mallee north of Birchip, towards Swan Hill. This is likely due to good winter crop growth, and a recent swing from long-term dry to wet conditions increases the risk of numbers rising further in coming seasons.
  - Elsewhere: Mouse numbers are expected to be low.

+ [New South Wales]{.underline}:
  - Mouse numbers are mostly low, except for possible moderate numbers in parts of the Liverpool Plains (near Gunnedah) and the eastern Riverina (near Temora and Wagga Wagga).

+ [Southern Queensland]{.underline}:
  - Mouse numbers are expected to be mostly low but with patches of moderate activity throughout. Estimated patchy moderate numbers are supported by reports of mouse damage and control action occuring in some places.
