+ [Western Australia]{.underline}: [**A mouse plague is under way across multiple regions**.]{style="color: red;"}
The worst outbreak is occurring north of Geraldton in the "WA Northern" agro-ecological zone, followed by another east from Fitzgerald in the "WA Sandplain". 
With crops maturing, consider baiting before grain fill, as zinc phosphide becomes less effective once grain develops on the head.
Mouse abundance in WA Eastern and Central zones appears to be low, but growers should remain vigilant. 

+ [Adelaide Plains, Yorke & Eyre Peninsulas (SA)]{.underline}: We have seen a decrease in mouse activity on the Adelaide Plains due to low food availaiblity and proactive management prior to sowing. 
Moderate mouse numbers still remain however, which could increase as more food is becoming available. Continue monitoring as crops mature, and be ready to act before grain fill if activity increases.

+ An emergency permit has been issued by the APVMA for the higher dose ZnP50 bait: see [GRDC website](https://grdc.com.au/resources-and-publications/resources/mouse-management) for details. 

+ Elsewhere (Mallee & Wimmera (Vic/SA), Victoria & southern/central NSW and Northern NSW & southern Queensland), mouse numbers likely remain low.
