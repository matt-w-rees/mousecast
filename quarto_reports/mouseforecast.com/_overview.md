+ [Western Australia]{.underline}: [**A mouse plague is under way across multiple regions**.]{style="color: red;"}
The worst outbreak is occurring in the "Northern" agro-ecological zone, followed by another east from Fitzgerald in the "Sandplain" zone. 
**Mouse activity should be monitored through winter. As crops start to mature, consider baiting before grain fill**, as zinc phosphide becomes less effective once grain develops in the head. 
Mouse abundance in WA Eastern and Central zones appears to be low, but growers should remain vigilant.

+ [South Australia]{.underline}: There is consistent moderate mouse activity across Adelaide Plains and the Yorke Peninsula. 
We have observed a slight decrease in activity, likely due to low food availability and proactive management prior to sowing. 
However, mouse activity could increase as more food becomes available. 
**Continue monitoring mice as crops mature, and be ready to act before grain fill if activity increases.**

+ [Elsewhere]{.underline}:: mouse numbers likely remain low.

+ An emergency permit has been issued by the APVMA for the higher dose ZnP50 bait: see [GRDC website](https://grdc.com.au/resources-and-publications/resources/mouse-management) for details.
